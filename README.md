# V2G---G2V-TECHNOLOGY
This project focuses on Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) technology, which enables bidirectional power flow between electric vehicles (EVs) and the electrical grid. It demonstrates two operating modes: Grid-to-Vehicle (G2V), where the EV battery is charged from the grid, and Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G), where stored battery energy is supplied back to the grid.
The project includes hardware implementation using transformers, rectifier circuits, voltage converters, lithium-ion batteries, and other electronic components. MATLAB/Simulink is also used for system modelling and simulation.
**Key Objectives:**
Demonstrate bidirectional energy transfer.
Understand G2V and V2G operating modes.
Explore battery charging and discharging.
Study the role of V2G in grid stability.
Understand the environmental benefits of reducing carbon emissions.
**Technologies Used:**
MATLAB, Simulink, Power Electronics, Transformers, Rectifier Circuits, and Lithium-Ion Batteries.
